A grammar of 22 verbs

A declaration is built from verbs, each for one thing a user does, grouped by activity. Perceive and trust hold no verb of their own: they run through every verb.

ActivityIts verbs
understandDISCOVER, UNDERSTAND, LOCATE, READ, SCAN, SCROLL, ZOOM, COMPARE
decideFOCUS, FILTER, SELECT, HIGHLIGHT, PREVIEW, MARK
actACT, CONFIRM
recoverCANCEL, UNDO, PAUSE, RESUME, RETRY, EXIT

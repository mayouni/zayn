The design system

Zayn designs every page from thirteen principles, each drawn from a measured fact about how a person perceives, attends and acts.

One principle, refused and corrected

The menu is the tree: a section's landing comes first in its band under a name of its own. A menu is a declaration too, so the verifier holds it to that principle.

This site's own menu is declared the same way, in site/menu.zayn, and the build refuses it on this rule.

Six facts the principles stand on

Each fact was measured long before these sites existed. A principle is kept only when it follows from one of them and the corpus of corrections shows a generator breaking it.

The factWhat it statesWhat follows
Grouping comes before readingThe eye groups what is near, alike, enclosed or connected before it reads a word.Space says what belongs together; a box or a line must draw a real group.
Every visible difference is read as a meaningA difference in position, size, colour or shape is taken as information, meant or not.A difference that means nothing is noise; one variable carries one meaning.
Attention and memory are scarceA person holds about four things at once, and each added choice costs time.One emphasis per element; a bar of five entries; decided stays decided.
Acting is a loop closed through what is perceivedA person finds a control by its sign, acts, then judges the result from what they see, hear or feel.One shape, one meaning; the menu is the tree; every input closes the loop.
Language is cooperativeA reader expects as much as needed, truthfully and plainly.Words for the reader, stated at their true size.
The senses have floorsBelow a size and a contrast, reading slows; below a size, a finger misses.Text set for reading; targets a finger can hit.

Thirteen principles, one for each cause of the corrections

Each principle closes one cause the corpus found. Each is shown live on the samples page, a lawful interface beside an offending one.

The principleIts bounds
Every text is set for readingSix sizes; a floor of 18 px; contrast of 7 to 1; reading text never shrinks to fit.
Width belongs to the contentA measure of sixty to seventy-five characters; a box at most a third empty; a control beside its content.
A line or a box draws something realLines only on tables, fields, drawings, focus, samples, code and the grid of peer regions.
One emphasis per element, one critical element per viewFive semantic colours and one neutral for data; motion only reports.
Balance is on by defaultA last line at least a third of the widest; no line crosses a sentence in a title.
One shape, one meaningFour shapes; a container never reacts.
The menu is the treeFive entries a bar; the landing first under its own label; an outline on longer pages.
Everything is on the page, with one scrollNo tooltip; nothing shown only under a pointer; no scroll inside the page; nothing moves the layout.
Every input reaches everythingEvery hover has a tap; targets of 48 px on a phone and 24 px elsewhere.
Words serve the readerA title of six words, a lede that defines; a live sample for every concept.
The page speaks for its readerAffirmative, at its true size; the action after the understanding.
The painted page is the instrumentRead on the painted page, at several widths and in both cultures.
Decided stays decidedNo platform default; the house's own components; a change named.

Each principle read on the painted page

The UI audit opens each page of a site in a browser and measures what was painted: sizes, contrast, widths, lines, targets and the menu's tree. A page passes on its paint, never on its source alone.
